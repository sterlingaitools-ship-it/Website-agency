## CHOSEN NICHE: trades-roofing-plumbing-electrical

### Why this one over the others

- **Highest ROI justification.** A single closed roofing job at $12,000 covers a $5k website investment in one transaction. The pitch to any roofer is a one-liner.
- **Lowest bar to impress.** The average roofing website loads in 6-8 seconds, has no trust stack, no phone CTA, and no review integration. A fast, proof-first, mobile-ready site outperforms 80% of the market without needing to be exceptional.
- **Systematizable prospecting.** Bad roofer websites are auditable from Google Maps in seconds. Aiden can build a list of 100 prospects with provably weak online presence in a weekend, and open every conversation with a specific, observable gap rather than a generic pitch.

### Niche customer reality check

- **They research before buying:** Yes. Homeowners check Google reviews, BBB, and certifications before calling. High-research, high-trust purchase.
- **Average ticket:** $12,000 (residential roof replacement, US market)
- **They hang out at:** Nextdoor, local Facebook groups, Google Search (not social media browsers - active searchers)
- **A $5k website ROI math:** 10 leads/month × 25% close rate × $12,000 average job = $30,000/month in new revenue. Website pays for itself in week one.

### 5 Example Businesses to Approach This Week

1. **Any roofer appearing on Google Maps in a US city with under 50 reviews** - search "roofer near me" using a US VPN or target a specific city (e.g. "roofer Atlanta GA"), filter by low review count. These businesses are visible, reachable, and provably underperforming online.
2. **Hook Agency client list gaps** - Hook Agency serves contractors doing $2M+. Any roofer under $2M in revenue is below their minimum. That is the exact addressable market.
3. **Angi/HomeAdvisor-listed roofers with no website** - search Angi for roofers in any US city, find the ones with no website link or a broken link. They are paying for leads and have no owned asset.
4. **Roofers with a Facebook page but no website** - search Facebook for "[city] roofing" and filter for businesses with active pages but no linked website. These owners are already trying to market online; they just haven't built the owned asset yet.
5. **Roofers who show up in Google Maps with a placeholder website or a template** - the "built with Wix" or "under construction" site is a direct signal that the owner knows they need something but hasn't acted. That is a warm conversation starter.
