## Top 3 Trust Signals to Lead With

These are the three elements that move homeowners from "maybe" to "I'm calling":

1. **Google review volume with star count.** 200+ reviews at 4.5+ stars. The number matters as much as the rating. If a roofer has 40 reviews, homeowners filter them out before clicking. Your job: surface that count above the fold.

2. **GAF Master Elite certification.** Less than 3% of US roofers qualify. When you add a GAF badge and explain in one sentence what it means ("Top 3% of US roofers — earns you a 50-year manufacturer warranty"), it functions as a hard quality filter. Most roofing sites have the badge but don't explain it.

3. **Named photo testimonials with specific outcomes.** Generic star ratings are expected and ignored. A photo, a full name, a city, and one outcome sentence ("They replaced our roof in one day and handled the entire insurance claim") converts. This is what you build into the Reviews section.
