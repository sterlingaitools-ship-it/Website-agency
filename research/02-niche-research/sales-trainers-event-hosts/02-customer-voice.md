## Language Patterns: 10 Most-Repeated Phrases

| Phrase | Pattern |
|---|---|
| "show me proof" | Demand for specific, verifiable results — not vague transformation language |
| "did they actually sell anything" | Credibility via personal track record, not just coaching credentials |
| "I never heard back" | Broken automation is the most common complaint post-signup |
| "the website looked outdated" | Website quality as proxy for trainer quality — a bad site kills trust before a word is read |
| "specific results" | Vague testimonials fail; specificity (role + number + timeframe) converts |
| "I was ready to buy" | Missed conversion window — follow-up gap costs sales |
| "who is this for" | Audience specificity — buyers want to know if the program fits their exact situation |
| "what will I actually learn" | Curriculum transparency is a buying signal trigger |
| "live vs recorded" | Format matters — live = accountability, recorded = flexible but lower trust |
| "I found a competitor" | Broken experience drives immediate churn to alternatives |

---

## Decision Moments

1. **Seeing a peer testimonial.** A specific result from someone in the same role/industry is the highest-converting trigger.
2. **Attending a free event or webinar.** The "give value first" model works. Buyers who experience the training method before committing are far more likely to upgrade.
3. **The follow-up sequence.** Buyers who signed up for a lead magnet and received a well-timed, relevant email sequence are converted at significantly higher rates.
4. **The Q&A moment.** When the buyer can ask a real question and receive a direct, useful answer — live or via chat — trust converts to purchase.
5. **Social proof of attendance.** Event photos with a packed room, social media posts, "sold out" signals all create FOMO and validate the buy decision.

---

## Top 5 Fears

1. **Wasting money on theory with no practical application.** Buyers have been burned by generic training that didn't change their actual numbers.
2. **The trainer has never actually done what they teach.** "Fake gurus" are a widely-discussed problem in the sales training space. Buyers are sceptical.
3. **The program is pre-recorded but sold as live.** Misleading format destroys trust when discovered.
4. **No accountability after purchase.** Buyers know they'll drift without someone holding them to implementation.
5. **Being sold a low ticket to be upsold into a high ticket.** The bait-and-switch funnel model is well-known and resented.
