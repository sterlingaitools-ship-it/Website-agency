# Sub-task 1: Existing Niche-Serving Agencies
## Niche: Sales Trainers and Event Hosts (US/UK)

---

### Funnel Creators
- **Approach:** Websites + sales funnels designed to bolster authority and supercharge conversions.
- **Pricing:** Not published.
- **Sells:** Authority-building and conversion-focused design.
- **Trust elements:** Portfolio of funnel builds.
- **What they miss:** No visible sales-training-specific positioning; generalist funnel shop.

### Bubblegum Marketing
- **Approach:** Full-service funnel agency — funnel design, automation, and ads management.
- **Pricing:** Not published. High-end positioning.
- **Sells:** Systematic client acquisition: attract, nurture, convert.
- **Trust elements:** Results framing, methodology language.
- **What they miss:** No sales trainer / event host specialisation visible. Targets coaches broadly.

### ClickX (White Label)
- **Approach:** White-label funnel design agency. Builds for agencies who then sell to coaches and trainers.
- **Pricing:** B2B wholesale pricing.
- **Sells:** Scalable funnel production to resellers.
- **Trust elements:** Agency client list.
- **What they miss:** Not positioned for direct outreach to sales trainers; operates upstream.

### Freelancers (Fiverr / Upwork)
- **Approach:** On-demand funnel and website builds. Variable quality.
- **Pricing:** $200 - $5,000+ per project depending on scope.
- **Sells:** Speed and price.
- **Trust elements:** Review ratings.
- **What they miss:** No relationship, no strategy, no ongoing support. Pure execution.

### GoHighLevel Community Agencies (unnamed, widespread)
- **Approach:** GHL-based agencies targeting coaches and online businesses. The single most saturated segment in this niche.
- **Pricing:** $500 - $3,000/month retainers common.
- **Sells:** GHL funnels, websites, automations, CRM.
- **Trust elements:** GHL platform credibility.
- **What they miss:** Undifferentiated. Hundreds of agencies sending identical cold DMs. No niche-within-niche positioning.

---

## Top 3 Patterns Winners Share

1. **Results with specificity.** Winners show specific numbers: "$2M in sales generated," "500 students enrolled," "3x conversion rate improvement." Vague claims ("high-converting funnels") are everywhere and ignored.
2. **Industry understanding in the copy.** The agencies that land sales trainers demonstrate they understand the world — discovery calls, SDRs, pipeline, close rates — not just website design. Speaking the client's language is a significant differentiator.
3. **Personal brand + case study combo.** The most effective agencies in this space pair a visible founder with 2-3 detailed case studies. Coaches trust coaches. If the agency owner presents as a savvy online marketer who has built their own funnel successfully, the credibility transfer is automatic.

## Top 3 Patterns Losers Share

1. **Generic "high-converting funnel" language.** Indistinguishable from 500 other GHL agencies. No specific angle, no niche, no result proof.
2. **No understanding of the buyer's world.** Agencies that say "I'll build you a website" to a sales trainer who knows what a funnel should do will be dismissed immediately. The buyer is sophisticated.
3. **Cold DM with no warm signal.** Mass cold outreach to coaches and trainers with no personalisation, no trigger (watched their event, bought their product, noticed their broken follow-up) is immediately deleted.
