# Sub-task 7: Money Math
## Niche: Sales Trainers and Event Hosts (US/UK)

---

## Average Ticket Size

| Offering | Average Price (USD) |
|---|---|
| Online sales course (pre-recorded) | $200 - $1,000 |
| Live virtual workshop / masterclass | $500 - $2,000 |
| In-person event / bootcamp (per ticket) | $500 - $5,000 |
| Group coaching programme (per cohort participant) | $3,000 - $6,000 |
| Corporate sales team training (per engagement) | $15,000 - $50,000+ |
| 1-on-1 high-ticket sales coaching | $5,000 - $25,000 |

**Working number for ROI pitch:** $3,500 per group coaching participant (mid-range, accessible, most common).

---

## Estimated Close Rate

| Lead source | Close rate |
|---|---|
| Webinar attendee → programme offer | 3 - 8% |
| Email list (warm, nurtured) | 1 - 5% |
| Discovery call booked | 20 - 40% |
| Paid ads → landing page | 1 - 3% |
| Referral / peer recommendation | 30 - 50% |

**Working number for website-sourced leads:** 5% from cold traffic who book a discovery call.

---

## Leads Needed Per Month

For a sales trainer targeting $30,000/month revenue:
- At $3,500 per participant: needs ~9 paying participants/month
- If selling a 10-person cohort format: needs 1 full cohort per month
- At 20% close rate on discovery calls: needs ~45 calls/month
- Website converting at 3%: needs ~1,500 monthly visitors

For event-based model (one event per quarter at $1,500/ticket, 50 attendees):
- Revenue: $75,000 per event
- Website role: registration page + email capture for re-marketing next event

---

## The One-Sentence Pitch to a Sales Trainer

"If your website and follow-up system convert even 5% of the leads already visiting your page, and your average group programme ticket is $3,500, getting 10 extra enrolments per month adds $35,000 in revenue — the website and automation pay for themselves in the first week."

---

## Website Pricing Justification

- Sales trainers and event hosts understand funnel math better than almost any other niche. The ROI conversation is easier because the buyer speaks the language.
- A $5,000 website + automation setup is a rounding error against a $50,000 event or a $30,000/month coaching operation.
- The harder sell is standing out: this buyer will evaluate Aiden's own website and positioning before buying. If Aiden's own funnel is sharp, the pitch closes itself.
- Agencies serving this niche charge $3,000 - $8,000 for website + funnel build, $1,000 - $3,000/month for retainer.
