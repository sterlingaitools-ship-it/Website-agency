# Sub-task 6: SEO Landscape
## Niche: Sales Trainers and Event Hosts (US/UK)

---

## Primary Keyword Cluster

| Keyword | Intent | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| sales training [city] | Transactional | City-specific for in-person events |
| online sales training | Transactional | High volume, high competition |
| sales coaching programme | Transactional | Higher intent, implies decision to buy |
| sales bootcamp [city/online] | Transactional | Event-format specific |
| B2B sales training | Transactional | Corporate buyer intent — higher ticket |

---

## Secondary / Long-Tail Clusters

| Cluster | Examples |
|---|---|
| Role-specific | "SDR training", "account executive coaching", "sales manager leadership training" |
| Industry-specific | "SaaS sales training", "real estate sales coaching", "insurance sales training" |

| Outcome-specific | "increase close rate", "how to hit quota consistently", "high ticket sales training" |

---

## Geographic Targets

This niche operates in two modes:
- **Local/in-person events:** City-specific SEO. Key markets: New York, London, Chicago, Los Angeles, Dallas, Atlanta, Manchester, Birmingham.
- **Online programmes:** National/international SEO. No geographic restriction. Compete on programme type and trainer authority.

For Aiden's client (a sales trainer or event host), the SEO strategy depends on their format. Most run both.

---

## Title and H1 Patterns (top-ranking pages)

**Title patterns:**
- `[Trainer Name] | Sales Training & Coaching for [Audience] | [City/Online]`
- `Sales Bootcamp [City] [Year] | [Trainer/Company Name]`
- `Online Sales Coaching Programme | [Outcome] in [Timeframe] | [Company]`

**H1 patterns:**
- `Close More Deals. Earn More. [Programme Name].`
- `The #1 Sales Training Programme for [Audience Type]`
- `[Trainer Name] — Sales Coach & Trainer | [City]`

---

## Schema Markup Commonly Used

- `Course` (structured data for training programmes — title, description, provider, price)
- `Event` (for in-person or virtual events — date, location, organiser, ticket price)
- `Person` (trainer authority — name, job title, same-as links to LinkedIn)
- `Review` / `AggregateRating` (testimonials and course ratings)
- `FAQPage` (objection-handling FAQs on sales pages)

---

## Content Gap Analysis

**Topics all ranking pages cover:**
- Programme / event landing page
- Trainer authority/about page
- Blog posts on sales tactics (prospecting, objection handling, closing)
- Free resource / lead magnet page

**Gaps that create differentiation:**
- Industry-vertical case studies ("How we trained the sales team at [Company Type] and increased close rate by X%")
- Role-specific content ("Sales training for SDRs specifically" vs. generic "sales training")
- Broken funnel audit content ("Why your leads aren't converting after signup — and how to fix it") — directly relevant to Aiden's skill set and a topic no other agency in this niche is writing about
- Event recap content with video snippets (compounds SEO and social proof simultaneously)
