## Common Ad Hook Structures

1. **"I signed up for [X] webinar and nothing happened after."** Addresses the broken funnel problem the buyer has already experienced with a competitor.
2. **"What [Big Name] teaches vs. what actually closes deals"** — contrarian positioning against established names.
3. **"This rep went from $60k to $220k in 14 months using this exact script."** Named result, specific numbers, time-bound.
4. **"Free workshop: the 5-step close that works even if you hate selling."** Free entry point + pain-point-specific benefit.
5. **"Only 12 spots left for [Month] cohort."** Scarcity trigger with specific limit.
