# Sub-task 5: Trust Signals
## Niche: Sales Trainers and Event Hosts (US/UK)

---

## Top 5 Trust Signals (priority order for buyers)

### 1. Specific Testimonials with Named Results
**Why #1:** Buyers in this niche have been burned by vague "this changed my life" testimonials. The signal that actually converts is: named person + role/industry + specific number + timeframe. Example: "After the 3-day bootcamp I closed my first $80,000 contract within 6 weeks. — Marcus D., B2B SaaS Account Executive, Chicago." The specificity is the proof. Vague is noise.

### 2. Trainer's Personal Sales Track Record
**Why #2:** The most frequent buyer fear is "has this person actually sold anything?" A trainer who can say "I closed over $2M in my first three years as an SDR at [Company]" or "I built and ran a 40-person sales team" carries instant authority that no certification can replicate. This must be in the hero or directly below it.

### 3. Volume of Students / Graduates Trained
**Why #3:** "500+ sales professionals trained" or "12 cohorts completed" signals that this is a real, ongoing programme — not a one-off experiment. Volume implies repeatability, repeatability implies a proven system.

### 4. Event Attendance Social Proof
**Why #4:** Photos and videos from packed events, standing ovations, networking moments. If the event exists and people showed up and stayed, the content is worth attending. Buyers use event photos as a quality proxy.

### 5. Money-Back Guarantee
**Why #5:** Reduces the financial risk of a $500-$5,000 purchase from a stranger on the internet. Standard in this niche. Not having one raises a question. The specificity of the guarantee matters: "if you complete all modules and don't see results in 90 days, we'll refund you" is more persuasive than a vague "satisfaction guarantee."

More relevant here than in trades. Buyers in the online training space respect media mentions (Forbes, Entrepreneur, Inc., LinkedIn Top Voice). A single Forbes article or LinkedIn featured post significantly increases conversion for cold traffic. Aiden's current LinkedIn newsletter is a starting point — if a client in this niche has been featured anywhere, it must be on the homepage.

## Photo / Video Testimonial Expectations

- **Video testimonials:** Highest-converting in this niche. A 60-90 second video from a graduate with a specific result before/after the programme is the gold standard.
- **Photo testimonials:** Expected. Should include headshot, full name, company/role, specific outcome.
- **Screenshot testimonials:** DMs, LinkedIn messages, and email screenshots showing real buyer reactions are commonly used as informal social proof (displayed in a collage format).
- **Live event photos:** Pack the "about the event" section with real crowd photos. Empty seats are never shown.
