# NICHE SYNTHESIS: sales-trainers-event-hosts

**Score from Module 2A:** 27/35

---

## End customer in one sentence

A sales professional or aspiring closer (25-45, employed or self-employed) who wants to earn more through better sales skills, is sceptical of generic gurus, and will only buy when they see a specific result from someone in a situation similar to their own.

## The student's client in one sentence

A sales trainer or event host (solo operator or small team) generating $5k-$50k/month, with strong content but a broken back-end — their funnel captures leads but loses them before conversion because the automation is either missing or incorrectly set up.

---

## Top 5 Insights

1. **The broken funnel is the pitch.** Aiden personally experienced the exact problem this niche has — signing up for events, giving an email, and never hearing back. He can open cold outreach with "I signed up for your event in [month] and never received a follow-up. Here's what you lost." That is a specific, non-generic opener that demonstrates the problem and the solution simultaneously.

2. **The buyer is sophisticated — which means the bar for the agency is higher.** A sales trainer knows what a funnel is supposed to do. A bad website or a clunky automation is not just an embarrassment — it actively undermines their authority with their own prospects. This buyer will scrutinise Aiden's pitch carefully. The pitch itself must be a demonstration of the skill being sold.

3. **Automation is the true product — the website is the wrapper.** In this niche, the website alone is not the value. The automation that fires when someone visits, signs up, attends a webinar, or drops off mid-checkout is the differentiating product. Aiden's CRM and automation background is directly applicable here and rare among generalist web agencies.

4. **Volume proof converts.** "500 students trained" or "12 cohorts completed" is what the buyer scans for first. Helping a client build and display this proof — through review aggregation, testimonial collection, and event recap content — is part of the product, not an add-on.

5. **The sophistication gap is real.** This niche is targeted by hundreds of GHL agencies sending identical cold DMs. A personalised, trigger-based outreach that demonstrates knowledge of the prospect's specific funnel gap will stand out dramatically against the background noise.

---

## Top 3 Risks If We Pick This Niche

- **The buyer evaluates your funnel before they buy.** If Aiden's own website, automation, and outreach sequence are not sharp, the credibility gap is fatal. The prospect will notice.
- **Crowded service provider market.** More GHL agencies target coaches and sales trainers than almost any other niche. Differentiation requires a very specific angle (broken automation diagnosis, not just website building).
- **Income claims and results require real proof.** When building websites for sales trainers, the testimonials and case studies must be real and specific. Thin proof will be noticed and will undermine the client's conversion.

---

## Top 3 Reasons to Pick This Niche

- **Aiden already has a direct insight from personal experience.** He registered for a sales event five times and never got follow-up. That story is the cold DM opening, the case study angle, and the product pitch all in one.
- **High ticket size with fast ROI justification.** At $3,500 per cohort participant, a website + automation that generates 10 extra enrolments per month delivers $35,000 in return. The buyer understands this math immediately.
- **Automation is the real differentiator.** Most web agencies in this space build the website and stop. Aiden's CRM segmentation and automation skill set turns a one-time project into an ongoing value relationship — and justifies a higher price point.
