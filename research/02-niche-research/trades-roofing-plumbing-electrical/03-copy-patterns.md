## Common Ad Hook Structures (Facebook/Meta patterns)

1. **Pain open:** "Is your roof leaking after last night's storm?" → solution → CTA
2. **Stat open:** "83% of homeowners overpay for roofing because they don't know what to ask" → education → CTA
3. **Social proof open:** "[Name] in [City] just saved $4,000 on their roof replacement. Here's how." → story → CTA
4. **Authority open:** "As a GAF Master Elite contractor, we're in the top 3% of roofers in the US." → credibility → CTA
5. **Urgency open:** "Storm season starts in 6 weeks. Is your roof ready?" → fear → inspection CTA
