## Geographic Targets

Roofing is hyper-local. The SEO strategy requires:
- One page per city/suburb the contractor serves
- Google Business Profile (GBP) optimised for each service area
- City-specific content (local weather events, permit requirements, city name in H1 and title)

High GBP density cities (strong existing competition, but large market):
- Houston TX, Atlanta GA, Phoenix AZ, Dallas TX, Denver CO, Nashville TN (US)
- For UK: Birmingham, Manchester, Leeds, Sheffield, Bristol (high trades demand)

## Schema Markup Commonly Used

- `LocalBusiness` (with `geo` coordinates and service area)
- `RoofingContractor` (specific business type)
- `Review` / `AggregateRating` (review count + average rating)
- `FAQPage` (common questions like "how long does roof replacement take?")
- `Service` (individual service pages)

---

## Content Gap Analysis

**Topics all ranking pages cover:**
- Service area pages (city-specific)
- Service-specific pages (replacement, repair, inspection, gutters)
- FAQs (cost, timeline, insurance, materials)
- About us / certifications page

**Gaps that create differentiation:**
- Insurance claim walkthrough content (step-by-step guide for homeowners)
- Roofing materials comparison guides (GAF vs Owens Corning vs CertainTeed)
- Local weather pattern pages ("How Texas hail affects roofing")
- Contractor verification guide ("How to avoid roofing scams in [City]") — turns the homeowner's fear into a trust-building asset
- Seasonal roof maintenance checklist (drives recurring search traffic)
