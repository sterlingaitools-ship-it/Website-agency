# Sub-task 5: Trust Signals
## Niche: Trades - Roofing, Plumbing, Electrical (US/UK)

---

## Top 5 Trust Signals (priority order for homeowners)

### 1. Volume Google Reviews with High Star Rating
**Why #1:** The homeowner's first action after finding a roofer is checking Google reviews. Sites with 200+ reviews at 4.5+ stars are filtered in automatically. Sites with fewer than 50 reviews or below 4.5 are filtered out before the homeowner even visits the website. This is the single highest-impact trust signal. The number matters as much as the rating.

### 2. Manufacturer Certification (GAF Master Elite / Owens Corning Platinum / CertainTeed SELECT ShingleMaster)
**Why #2:** Less than 3% of US roofing contractors qualify as GAF Master Elite. When a homeowner sees this badge (and the site explains what it means), it functions as a hard third-party quality filter. It also enables the contractor to offer the manufacturer's extended warranty — a direct differentiator. Owens Corning Platinum and CertainTeed SELECT ShingleMaster serve the same role. For the website: display the badge prominently and add one sentence explaining what it means to a homeowner who has never heard of it.

### 3. BBB Accreditation (A or A+ Rating)
**Why #3:** The BBB is the homeowner's instinctive trust proxy for trades. Many homeowners check BBB before calling. An A+ badge on the website signals the contractor resolves disputes, maintains ethical practices, and has longevity. Displaying the badge with a link to the actual BBB profile adds credibility.

### 4. License Number Displayed Openly
**Why #4:** Displaying the state contractor license number signals nothing to hide. In states where roofing requires a license, homeowners are told to verify. Contractors who list their number on the site remove a friction point. Those who don't raise a question in the homeowner's mind.

### 5. Named Photo Testimonials with Specific Outcomes
**Why #5:** Generic star ratings are expected. Named testimonials with a photo and a specific outcome ("They replaced our roof in one day and handled the entire insurance claim. — Sarah T., Plano TX") convert. The specificity (name, location, outcome) makes the testimonial believable. Video testimonials are the gold standard and rare enough to be a real differentiator.

## Niche-Specific Certifications and Badges

| Certification | Significance |
|---|---|
| GAF Master Elite | Top 3% of US roofers. Enables the Golden Pledge warranty (50-year coverage). |
| GAF Certified Contractor | Entry-level GAF certification. More common, less powerful than Master Elite. |
| Owens Corning Platinum Preferred | Similar to GAF Master Elite for Owens Corning materials. |
| CertainTeed SELECT ShingleMaster | CertainTeed's premium contractor tier. |
| BBB Accreditation (A+) | Third-party dispute resolution and ethics track record. |
| NRCA (National Roofing Contractors Association) Member | Industry body membership — signals professionalism. |
| State Contractor License | Required in most states. Displaying the number builds trust. |
| OSHA Safety Certified Crew | Becoming more common. Appeals to risk-conscious homeowners. |

## Press / "As Seen In" Expectations

Not a standard trust signal in trades. Local press (neighbourhood Facebook groups, local news coverage after storms, Nextdoor recommendations) carries more weight than national media. A "Recommended by Nextdoor Neighbours" badge or screenshot is more persuasive in this niche than most press mentions.

---

## Photo / Video Testimonial Expectations

- **Photo testimonials:** Expected and standard. Named + photo + location + specific outcome.
- **Video testimonials:** Rare in roofing. A 60-second video from a real homeowner talking about the experience is a significant differentiator. Most roofing sites do not have them.
- **Before/after galleries:** Not technically testimonials, but function as proof of quality. High-performing sites show 10-20 project photos with location tags.
