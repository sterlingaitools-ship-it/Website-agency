## Top 3 patterns winners share

1. **Niche-only positioning.** The strongest agencies serve only roofing (or only contractors) and make that loudly clear. Generalists are filtered out by savvy buyers.
2. **Volume credibility.** "1,000+ roofing websites" or "150+ roofing companies" signals safety. Buyers want proof of repeatability, not one great case study.
3. **Results framing, not feature listing.** Winners talk about leads generated, revenue milestones reached, and time-to-result. Losers list services (SEO, PPC, web design).

## Top 3 patterns losers share

1. **No visible proof.** No named clients, no hard numbers, no before/after metrics. Generic "we build great websites" copy.
2. **Multi-niche dilution.** Serving plumbers, roofers, HVAC, landscapers, and dentists undermines the authority that niche-only agencies command.
3. **Feature-first positioning.** Leading with "mobile responsive," "fast loading," "SEO optimised" instead of outcomes (leads, calls, revenue).
