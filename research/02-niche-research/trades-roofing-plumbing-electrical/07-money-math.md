# Sub-task 7: Money Math
## Niche: Trades - Roofing, Plumbing, Electrical (US/UK)

---

## Average Ticket Size

| Service | Average Job Value (US) |
|---|---|
| Residential roof replacement | $10,000 - $20,000 |
| Roof repair | $500 - $3,000 |
| Storm damage / insurance claim | $8,000 - $25,000 |
| Gutter replacement | $1,000 - $3,000 |
| Commercial roofing | $20,000 - $100,000+ |

**Working number for ROI pitch:** $12,000 per residential roof replacement.

---

## Estimated Close Rate

| Lead source | Close rate |
|---|---|
| Organic SEO (website) | 30 - 40% |
| Google Ads | 15 - 25% |
| Facebook Ads | 10 - 20% |
| Shared lead services (Angi, HomeAdvisor) | 2 - 5% |

**Working number for website-sourced leads:** 25% close rate.

---

## Leads Needed Per Month

For a roofer targeting $50,000/month in revenue:
- At $12,000 average ticket: needs ~4 closed jobs/month
- At 25% close rate: needs ~16 qualified leads/month
- Website converting at 5% (industry average): needs ~320 monthly visitors

For a roofer targeting $100,000/month:
- Needs ~8 closed jobs/month
- At 25% close rate: needs ~32 qualified leads/month

---

## The One-Sentence Pitch to a Roofer

"If your website brings you 10 qualified leads a month and you close 3 of them at your average job size of $12,000, that's $36,000 in new revenue — your website pays for itself in the first month and keeps paying every month after."

---

## Website Pricing Justification

- A $5,000 website that generates 3 closed jobs at $12,000 each returns $36,000 in its first month.
- Roofing is one of the few niches where a single closed lead covers the entire website cost.
- Agencies in this space charge $3,000 - $10,000 for website-only, $1,500 - $7,500/month for retainer (SEO + PPC + web).
- Aiden's entry price point of $3,000 - $5,000 for website + basic automation is well within market range and easy to justify on ROI.
