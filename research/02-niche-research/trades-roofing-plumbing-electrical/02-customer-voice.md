## Language Patterns: 10 Most-Repeated Phrases

| Phrase | Pattern |
|---|---|
| "near me" | Location attachment - searching for local proximity above all else |
| "licensed and insured" | Minimum bar - non-negotiable filter before anything else |
| "free estimate / free quote" | Low-friction entry point - price is unknown and scary |
| "five-star reviews" | Plural social proof - volume matters, not just rating |
| "storm damage" | Urgency trigger - insurance-related projects are high-anxiety |
| "I didn't know who to trust" | Core fear statement - trust is the entire purchase decision |
| "they showed up" | Reliability signal - the bar is embarrassingly low; just showing up wins |
| "warranty" | Risk transfer - homeowners want the risk off their plate |
| "get a free inspection" | Entry point CTA - inspection before quote lowers the perceived commitment |
| "certified" | Authority shortcut - they don't know what certifications mean but trust them anyway |

## Decision Moments

1. **Roof leaks or storm damage hits** - forced decision, high urgency, low time to research.
2. **Getting 3 quotes** (standard advice from family/friends) - whoever has the most credible online presence wins the comparison.
3. **Insurance adjuster is involved** - homeowner hands off to the roofer to deal with the claim; they want someone who has done this before.
4. **Neighbour just had their roof done** - social proof from the street triggers the decision to finally address the ageing roof.
5. **Spring/fall seasonal triggers** - after winter damage or before next winter; planning-mode homeowners research during these windows.

---

## Top 5 Fears

1. **Getting ripped off or scammed.** Fly-by-night contractors are a well-known problem. Every homeowner has a horror story or knows someone who does.
2. **Shoddy work causing more damage.** A bad roof installation can cause water damage, mould, and structural issues. The stakes are high.
3. **Not knowing what they're paying for.** Roofing is opaque. Homeowners feel powerless because they can't evaluate quality themselves.
4. **The contractor disappearing mid-job.** Deposit paid, work half-done, contractor vanishes. This is a real pattern and homeowners know it.
5. **Insurance headaches.** If it's a storm/insurance claim, they fear the contractor and insurer will not agree, leaving them stuck in the middle.
