## Direction A: Proof-First Authority

**Archetype:** Lead with proof. Phone number in nav. Review count + GAF badge + BBB badge above the fold. Quote form embedded in the hero beside the image. Navy/white color system with high-contrast orange CTA.

**Representative sites:** Cloud Roofing (cloudroofing.com), Centennial Roofing (centennialroofing.com), Hometown Roofing (hometownroofing.com)

### Scores

| Category | Score | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| CTA visibility above fold | 14/15 | "Get Your Free Estimate" button is primary color, above fold. Phone number repeated in nav and hero. Loses 1: form is in hero but secondary on mobile. |
| Trust signal density | 15/15 | Review count + star rating, GAF certification badge, BBB A+ badge, and license number all visible without scrolling. Trust bar immediately below hero adds 1-2 more signals. |
| Hero clarity | 14/15 | Headline follows "X Five-Star Reviews. [City]'s Most Trusted Roofer." pattern — specific, verifiable, no fluff. Subhead names location + certifications. Loses 1: headline is heavy (3 claims stacked). |
| Mobile pattern | 9/10 | Sticky nav with phone. Trust bar stacks cleanly. Loses 1: form fields require scrolling on mobile (not in fixed viewport). |
| Visual coherence | 13/15 | Navy/white system with orange accent is high-contrast and clean. Real crew photo in hero. Loses 2: trust bar badges often inconsistent in size on sites using this pattern. |
| Navigation | 10/10 | Service links + "Free Estimate" in nav. Phone number right-aligned. Sticky on scroll. |
| Form friction | 9/10 | 4-field form (Name, Phone, Service, Message) in hero. Clear submit CTA. Loses 1: 4 fields is one too many for highest conversion. |
| Conversion confidence | 9/10 | End customer arrives, sees reviews count + certification, phone number, and free estimate offer all above fold. Loses 1: certification badge has no explanatory text. |
| **Total** | **93/100** | |

### Distinctive moves
- Trust bar immediately below hero (no need to scroll to see proof)
- Phone number in nav bar + repeated as text link in hero
- Review count in hero subtext (e.g. "312 five-star reviews")
- GAF Master Elite badge with brief "top 3% of US roofers" explanation

### Anti-patterns on typical implementations
- Certification badges without explanatory copy (homeowner doesn't know what GAF means)
- Form with 5+ fields kills mobile conversion
- Trust bar badges pixelated or inconsistent sizing

## Direction B: Emergency-Led Action

**Archetype:** Lead with urgency and availability. "24/7" as the first word in the hero. High-contrast dark hero with single-field phone capture. Mobile bottom bar with tap-to-call. Accent: bright orange or red. Secondary proof bar below fold.

**Representative sites:** Edge 2 Edge Roofing (edge2edgeroofing.com), Bone Dry Roofing (bonedryroofing.com)

### Scores

| Category | Score | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| CTA visibility above fold | 15/15 | "Call Now" button + phone number are the entire hero. Single action, no competing elements. |
| Trust signal density | 10/15 | Reviews and certifications appear below fold in this pattern. Homeowners who arrive via emergency search don't scroll. Trust bar placement is the weakness. |
| Hero clarity | 13/15 | "Emergency Roof Repair Available 24/7" is crystal clear for the storm-damage searcher. Loses 2: wrong pattern for non-emergency ("Get a Free Estimate") traffic from organic search. |
| Mobile pattern | 10/10 | Fixed bottom bar with "Call Now" tap-to-call on mobile. Best mobile CTA pattern in the niche. |
| Visual coherence | 12/15 | Dark overlay hero with high-contrast accent works for urgency. Loses 3: dark scheme is harder to maintain visual quality in services grid and gallery sections. |
| Navigation | 8/10 | Simplified nav for urgency — loses 2 because service links are buried in a hamburger on desktop, reducing browse-path conversion. |
| Form friction | 7/10 | One-field phone capture is low friction but misses "what service" context, reducing lead quality. |
| Conversion confidence | 8/10 | Strong for emergency intenders, weak for research-mode homeowners (the larger segment). |
| **Total** | **83/100** | |

### Distinctive moves
- Mobile fixed bottom bar (best in class for click-to-call)
- Minimal hero clutter — one action only
- "24/7" as the lead trust signal (speed + availability)

### Anti-patterns on typical implementations
- Trust signals buried below fold means research-mode homeowners leave without converting
- Dark scheme poorly maintained across all sections
- One-field capture produces low-quality leads (no service type context)

## Direction C: Story-Driven Community

**Archetype:** Lead with local identity. "Serving [City] Since [Year]" headline. Photo-heavy hero with real crew. Named testimonials with photos are a prominent mid-page section. Process walkthrough is high-priority. Navy/white/grey system, warmer accent color (forest green or burgundy). Trust badges present but secondary to story.

**Representative sites:** Weathertight Corp (weathertightcorp.com), Premier Roofing (premieroofing.com), Champion Roofing (championroofing.com)

### Scores

| Category | Score | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| CTA visibility above fold | 11/15 | "Get Your Free Quote" button present but competes with story-forward headline. Phone is visible in nav but not repeated in hero. |
| Trust signal density | 12/15 | Certifications present but below fold. Google review widget mid-page. Strong for research-mode visitors who scroll; weak for above-fold proof. |
| Hero clarity | 12/15 | "Serving [City] Since [Year]" is clear on emotional resonance but lacks the quantified proof of Direction A. |
| Mobile pattern | 7/10 | No fixed bottom bar. Trust bar stacks but long scroll before conversion offer repeated. |
| Visual coherence | 14/15 | Strongest visual consistency. Warmer accent + real photography creates the highest trust aesthetic. |
| Navigation | 9/10 | Service links well-structured. Loses 1: "Free Estimate" nav CTA sometimes absent in this pattern. |
| Form friction | 8/10 | Form on dedicated Contact page, not hero. Reduces impulse conversion. |
| Conversion confidence | 9/10 | High for research-mode homeowners who scroll. Lower for scan-and-leave mobile users. |
| **Total** | **82/100** | |

### Distinctive moves
- Named photo testimonials with specific outcomes (highest persuasion format in the niche)
- "Serving [City] Since [Year]" headline taps community identity
- Before/after gallery section prominent mid-page

### Anti-patterns on typical implementations
- Form on a separate contact page adds unnecessary click to conversion
- Story-first above fold means proof is below fold — wrong for homeowners who are fear-driven
- No fixed mobile CTA (biggest conversion gap)

---

## Recommended base: Direction A

Direction A scores highest against the end-customer conversion rubric. The homeowner's primary fear is being scammed. Proof above the fold — review count, certification, license number, phone number — is the single most effective structural response to that fear. Directions B and C optimize for narrower scenarios.

A hybrid is possible: Direction A as the base with Direction B's mobile fixed bottom bar added (it doesn't conflict with A's structure) and Direction C's named photo testimonials replacing generic star-rating widgets.

---

## Anti-pattern reference (applies across all directions)

From Module 2B research, patterns that actively hurt conversion in this niche:
- Phone number not visible above fold on mobile
- No quote form in the hero (routing users to a separate contact page)
- Generic stock photography (crew in hard hats standing in front of a house — not real work)
- No manufacturer certification badge in the trust bar
- Trust bar below the fold (homeowners who scan-and-leave never see proof)
- "Submit" as form button text (first-person alternatives convert higher: "Get My Free Quote")
