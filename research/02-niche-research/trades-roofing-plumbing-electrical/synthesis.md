# NICHE SYNTHESIS: trades-roofing-plumbing-electrical

**Score from Module 2A:** 30/35

---

## End customer in one sentence

A homeowner (35-65, homeowner for 5+ years) who has just experienced roof damage or is planning a replacement, is terrified of being scammed, and will choose the contractor with the most visible proof of trust before they call anyone.

## The student's client in one sentence

A small roofing contractor (1-10 crew, $500k-$5M annual revenue) with no website or a poor one, spending on Angi/HomeAdvisor shared leads, who knows they need a better online presence but has no time or knowledge to build it.

---

## Top 5 Insights

1. **Trust is the entire purchase decision.** The homeowner does not know how to evaluate roof quality. They evaluate the contractor's credibility instead. A website that leads with proof (review count, certifications, license number) wins before the first phone call.

2. **Phone number is the primary CTA.** Unlike most niches, the homeowner's instinct is to call, not fill a form. Phone number must be in the nav bar, above the fold, and click-to-call on mobile. Removing this friction is as important as any copy element.

3. **Insurance claim assistance is an underused differentiator.** Contractors who say "we handle the insurance claim for you" on their homepage convert better on storm-damage leads. Most roofer websites do not have this positioned prominently.

4. **The bar for "winning" online is low.** The average roofing website loads in 6-8 seconds, has poor mobile UX, and no trust stack. A clean, fast, proof-first site with a phone number and Google review integration will outperform 80% of the market without extraordinary effort.

5. **Volume social proof beats quality social proof.** 200 four-star reviews outperforms 10 five-star reviews in homeowner decision-making. The client's first job is to get reviews flowing. The website's job is to display them loudly.

---

## Top 3 Risks If We Pick This Niche

- **Cold outreach into a crowded agency market.** Roofing is the most popular niche for contractor-focused agencies. Standing out to roofers who are already bombarded requires a very sharp cold DM angle.
- **Geographic complexity at scale.** Each city needs its own SEO page. Building for one contractor is manageable; scaling to multiple clients across markets requires a templated local SEO system.
- **Seasonal revenue for the client.** Roofing is weather-dependent. Clients in colder markets slow down in winter, which could affect their willingness to pay retainers in off-season.

---

## Top 3 Reasons to Pick This Niche

- **Highest ROI justification of any niche.** A $12,000 average job means one closed lead covers a $5k website. The pitch writes itself.
- **Templateable website structure.** Every roofing site needs the same sections: hero with proof, trust bar, services, process, reviews, gallery, service areas, CTA. One well-built template works for 90% of clients.
- **Low bar to impress.** The average roofing website is terrible. A fast, mobile-first, proof-led site is a dramatic upgrade for most contractors and produces visible results quickly — which means referrals and case studies come fast.

---

## Money Math Headline

"If your website brings 10 qualified leads a month and you close 3 at your average job size of $12,000, that's $36,000 in new revenue — the website pays for itself in the first month and keeps paying every month after."
