# Sub-task 4: CRO Patterns That Win
## Niche: Trades - Roofing, Plumbing, Electrical (US/UK)

---

## Most Common Section Sequence (top-performing roofing sites)

1. Hero (headline + subhead + phone CTA + quote form or button)
2. Trust bar (BBB badge, GAF badge, review count, years in business)
3. Services grid (roof replacement, repair, inspection, storm damage, gutters)
4. Why us / differentiators (licensed, insured, local, warranty, 24/7)
5. Process (3-step: call → inspection → install)
6. Reviews / testimonials (Google reviews widget or screenshots with names)
7. Recent projects / before and after gallery
8. Service areas (map or city list)
9. Final CTA section (repeat the offer — free estimate)
10. Footer (phone, license number, address, links)

---

## Hero Composition

- **Imagery subject:** Crew on a roof actively working, or completed high-quality roof with suburban home in background. Real photos outperform stock every time.
- **Primary CTA:** "Get a Free Estimate" or "Get a Free Quote" — always above the fold, always a button (not a link).
- **Secondary CTA:** Phone number with click-to-call. Prominently displayed. Often in the nav bar.
- **Social proof in hero:** Review count ("200+ five-star reviews"), certification badge, or "serving [City] since [Year]" placed near or inside the hero.
- **Quote form:** High-converting sites put a multi-field form directly in the hero (Name, Phone, Email, Service Needed). Converts better than routing to a separate contact page.

---

## Trust Stack Ordering

1. Review count + rating (Google/BBB star display) — first trust signal
2. Manufacturer certifications (GAF Master Elite, Owens Corning Platinum) — authority
3. BBB accreditation badge — third-party validation
4. License number (displayed openly) — compliance signal
5. Years in business — longevity proof
6. Insurance confirmation — risk removal
7. Named photo testimonials — human social proof
8. Before/after project gallery — work quality proof

---

## Form Patterns

- **Fields:** Name, phone, email, service type (dropdown), optional message. 4-5 fields standard.
- **Form placement:** In the hero (best), also repeated in the footer CTA section.
- **Form headline:** "Get Your Free Estimate" or "Request a Free Inspection."
- **Submit button text:** "Get My Free Quote" — first-person language outperforms "Submit."
- **Mobile:** Full-width tap-to-call button replaces or supplements the form on mobile.

---

## Sticky Elements

- **Sticky nav bar:** Phone number always visible as user scrolls.
- **Mobile CTA bar:** Floating bottom bar on mobile with tap-to-call button. Standard on high-performing sites.
- **Exit intent:** Less common in trades; the conversion mechanic is phone/form, not email capture.

---

## Divergence from Universal Wireframe

The standard universal wireframe puts testimonials mid-page after the value props. Roofing sites push social proof signals (review count, badges) into the hero and the trust bar immediately below it. The homeowner fear of scams means trust must be established before the homeowner reads anything else. Leads with proof, not with the sales pitch.

The other divergence: roofing sites universally include a phone number in the header/nav bar. Most industries do not. For roofing, the phone number IS the primary CTA because the homeowner's instinct is to call, not to fill a form.
