## Top 3 Patterns Winners Share

1. **Fitness-only or coach-only positioning.** The agencies winning in this space have dropped all other niches. "We build websites for personal trainers" is more trusted than "we build websites for small businesses."
2. **Portfolio of real trainer sites.** Buyers in this niche want to see websites that look like what they want. A strong portfolio gallery on the agency homepage converts. Quantity (20+ examples) plus quality (professional, conversion-optimised) wins.
3. **Before/after or results framing.** The agencies that convert best show what the client's online presence looks like before their intervention vs. after — in terms of both aesthetics and outcomes (leads, bookings, revenue).

## Top 3 Patterns Losers Share

1. **Generic "we build beautiful websites" copy.** Fitness coaches hear this from every agency. The differentiator must be specific to fitness: "we build websites that fill your client roster."
2. **No booking integration.** Fitness coach websites that do not include a frictionless booking flow miss the primary purpose. Losers build brochure sites; winners build client-acquisition systems.
3. **Ignoring mobile.** Most fitness clients find trainers on their phones. A desktop-first build that looks broken on mobile loses the client before they read anything.
