## Language Patterns: 10 Most-Repeated Phrases

| Phrase | Pattern |
|---|---|
| "real results from real people" | Authenticity demand — generic before/afters are suspected of being fake |
| "someone who looks like me" | Relatability — buyers want proof from a peer, not an aspirational outlier |
| "certified / what cert" | Credential check — certification is the minimum bar, not a differentiator |
| "book online" | Friction removal — phone/email enquiry creates enough friction to lose the lead |
| "near me" | Local intent dominant even for online coaches who have physical location SEO |
| "before/after photos" | The single highest-converting visual in this niche |
| "pricing wasn't on the site" | Price opacity creates friction — many buyers abandon without enquiring |
| "they specialised in [goal]" | Specificity converts — "weight loss for busy mums" beats "general fitness coaching" |
| "friend recommended" | Referral is still the highest-converting channel; the website must make sharing easy |
| "felt like I already knew them" | Personal connection via video or authentic copy reduces the stranger risk |

## Decision Moments

1. **Before/after photo recognition.** Seeing a transformation from someone who looks like the buyer triggers the "if they can, I can" response — the highest-intent moment before a purchase.
2. **Friend/peer recommendation.** Word-of-mouth with a first-person endorsement converts immediately. The website's job is to confirm the recommendation, not generate the trust from scratch.
3. **Free consultation booking.** Low-friction entry — the buyer doesn't commit to money, just time. The call converts if the trainer can demonstrate understanding of the buyer's specific goal.
4. **Google search at a decision point.** "Personal trainer near me" at the moment the buyer has decided to make a change (January, after a health scare, after a big life event). First credible result with strong reviews wins.
5. **Video first impression.** A 60-second intro video from the trainer creates immediate personal connection. Buyers who watch the video and feel they "like" the trainer convert at significantly higher rates.

---

## Top 5 Fears

1. **Wasted money on a trainer who doesn't actually deliver results.** Fitness coaching is easy to fake. Buyers are cautious because the market is saturated with underqualified trainers.
2. **Generic programming that isn't designed for them.** "I don't want a cookie-cutter plan from someone who gives every client the same thing."
3. **Hard sell pressure after a free trial.** Many buyers have experienced the gym membership upsell trap and are wary of entering a funnel.
4. **Commitment to a long contract with no results guarantee.** Paying upfront for 3-6 months and seeing no results is a real fear.
5. **Trainer cancellations and inconsistency.** Buyers have been burned by trainers who reschedule constantly. A professional website signals a professional operation.
