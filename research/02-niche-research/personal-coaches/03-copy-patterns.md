## Common Ad Hook Structures

1. **Before/after scroll stop:** Silent video transitioning from a client's before state to after. No text needed for the hook — the visual does the work.
2. **"If you've tried everything and still can't..."** — calls out the exhausted buyer who has failed with generic approaches.
3. **Trainer's personal story open:** "I gained 30lbs during the pandemic. Here's how I got it back — and how I help my clients do the same."
4. **Social proof hook:** "[Client name] lost 22lbs in 10 weeks working with me. Here's exactly what we did."
5. **Objection removal hook:** "You don't need 2 hours in the gym every day. This works in 45 minutes, 3 times a week."
