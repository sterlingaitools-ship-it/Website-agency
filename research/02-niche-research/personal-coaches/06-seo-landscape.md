# Sub-task 6: SEO Landscape
## Niche: Personal Coaches - Fitness, Nutrition, Wellness (US/UK)

---

## Primary Keyword Cluster

| Keyword | Intent | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| personal trainer near me | Transactional/Local | Highest local intent. Google Maps pack dominates. |
| online personal trainer | Transactional | Growing rapidly post-COVID. High competition. |
| personal trainer [city] | Transactional | City-specific pages essential. |
| online fitness coach | Transactional | Broader than PT — includes nutrition and lifestyle coaching. |
| weight loss coach [city/online] | Transactional | Specific outcome — high buyer intent. |

---

## Secondary / Long-Tail Clusters

| Cluster | Examples |
|---|---|
| Audience-specific | "personal trainer for women over 40", "PT for beginners", "coach for busy mums/dads" |
| Goal-specific | "how to lose weight at home", "muscle building coach", "marathon training coach" |

| Credential-specific | "NASM certified personal trainer [city]", "REPs Level 4 PT [city]" |
| Platform/format | "personal training via Zoom", "fitness app coaching", "Whatsapp fitness coaching" |

---

## Geographic Targets

Local personal trainers need:
- Google Business Profile (GBP) optimised with services, photos, review solicitation
- City + neighbourhood-specific landing pages
- "near me" content strategy

Online coaches need:
- Audience-specific content (who they serve, not where they are)
- Long-form content around common client questions
- Social SEO (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) as discovery supplements to Google

High-demand markets:
- US: New York, Los Angeles, Miami, Chicago, Austin, Houston, Atlanta
- UK: London, Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, Edinburgh

---

## Title and H1 Patterns (top-ranking pages)

**Title patterns:**
- `Personal Trainer [City] | [Trainer Name] | [Credential] | Free Consultation`
- `Online Personal Trainer | [Audience Type] | [Trainer Name]`
- `[Audience-Specific Outcome] Coach | [City/Online] | [Trainer Name]`

**H1 patterns:**
- `[City]'s Top-Rated Personal Trainer — [Number] Five-Star Reviews`
- `Online Fitness Coaching for [Specific Audience]`
- `[Trainer Name] | Personal Trainer in [City]`

---

## Schema Markup Commonly Used

- `LocalBusiness` (with `geo`, service area, phone)
- `Person` (trainer profile — name, job title, certification, same-as social links)
- `Service` (individual services — personal training, nutrition coaching, online coaching)
- `Review` / `AggregateRating`
- `FAQPage` (common objections — pricing, credentials, results timeframe)
- `Event` (for live challenges, bootcamps, or group programmes)

---

## Content Gap Analysis

**Topics all ranking pages cover:**
- Service pages (in-person, online, nutrition, specific programmes)
- About page with credentials and personal story
- Testimonials / results page
- FAQ page

**Gaps that create differentiation:**
- Audience-specific blog content ("How to lose weight after 40" ranks with low competition and targets the exact buyer)
- Comparison content ("Online personal trainer vs. gym PT — which is right for you")
- Nutrition + training combined content (most PT sites ignore nutrition; clients think about both)
- Client journey documentation (blog posts following a client from week 1 to week 12 — real, ongoing social proof that doubles as SEO content)
- Local fitness community content (event roundups, local gym reviews) — signals local relevance to Google
