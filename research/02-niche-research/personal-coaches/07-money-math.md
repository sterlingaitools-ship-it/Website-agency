# Sub-task 7: Money Math
## Niche: Personal Coaches - Fitness, Nutrition, Wellness (US/UK)

---

## Average Ticket Size

| Service | Average Price (USD) |
|---|---|
| Single PT session (in-person) | $50 - $120 per hour |
| Monthly retainer (in-person, 8 sessions) | $400 - $800/month |
| Monthly retainer (online coaching, check-ins + programming) | $150 - $500/month |
| 12-week transformation programme (online) | $500 - $2,000 |
| 12-week programme (premium/1-on-1 online) | $2,000 - $5,000 |
| Group online coaching (per participant) | $97 - $500/month |

**Working number for ROI pitch (online coach):** $500/month per client.
**Working number for in-person:** $600/month per client (8 sessions at $75/session).
**Annual LTV per retained client:** $5,000 - $7,200 (if retained 10-12 months).

| Social media DM | 5 - 15% |

**Working number for website-sourced leads:** 20% close rate on free consultation bookings.

---

## Leads Needed Per Month

For a personal trainer targeting $5,000/month revenue:
- At $500/month per online client: needs 10 active clients
- To acquire 2 new clients/month (assuming 20% monthly churn): needs 10 qualified leads/month
- At 20% close rate on consultations: needs 10 consultation calls/month
- Website converting at 5% to consultation booking: needs 200 monthly visitors

For growth-mode coach targeting $10,000/month:
- Needs 20 active clients or 10 at $1,000/month
- 4 new clients/month needed to cover churn and growth
- Needs 20 consultation calls/month
- Website needs 400+ monthly visitors at 5% conversion

---

## The One-Sentence Pitch to a Personal Coach

"If your website converts even 5% of visitors into a free consultation, and you close 2 out of every 10 consultations at $500 a month per client, getting 200 extra people to your site per month adds $2,000 in monthly recurring revenue — your website pays for itself in the first month and builds from there."

---

## Website Pricing Justification

- A $3,000 - $5,000 website for a personal trainer generating 2 new clients at $500/month returns $1,000/month recurring — the website pays for itself in 3-5 months.
- For a premium online coach at $2,000 per 12-week programme, one extra client per month covers the website in a single transaction.
- The challenge: personal trainers are often price-sensitive (their own margins are moderate). The sweet spot for website pricing in this niche is $1,500 - $3,500 for a build, with optional $300-$500/month for maintenance + automation.
- Agencies serving this niche charge $1,000 - $4,000 for website builds, with some charging $2,000 - $5,000 for full funnel + booking system integration.
