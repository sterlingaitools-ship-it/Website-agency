# SOP 05 - Copy Deck

Implements: Stage 6.

## Purpose

Write the complete site copy for a roofing contractor client in the niche voice: direct, plain, trust-first. Every sentence earns homeowner trust before asking for any action. The homeowner is fear-driven — they are worried about being overcharged or choosing an unreliable contractor. Copy must address that fear with specific, verifiable proof rather than vague claims.

## Procedure

1. Read copy-locks.json. These strings are fixed: form header "Get Your Free Estimate", submit button "Get My Free Quote", privacy line "No spam. No obligation. Your info stays private." Do not alter them.
2. Read brand-dna.json to extract: company name, city, founded year, review count, certifications (GAF tier, BBB status), license number, and any named services.
3. Write homepage copy in this order: hero headline (proof-first pattern), hero subhead (city + certifications + year), hero form header (locked), trust bar labels (5 items), services section heading + per-service one-line description, why-us section heading + 4 differentiator lines, process section heading + 3 step descriptions, reviews section heading, gallery section heading, service areas heading, final CTA heading + subhead.
4. Write one service page per service in the sitemap: unique H1 following "[Service] in [City] | [Company Name]" pattern, 200-word body (problem + solution + proof + CTA), FAQ block (3 questions minimum).
5. Write one city page per service area in the sitemap: unique H1, 150-word body with city name woven in naturally, local trust signals, CTA.
6. Write About page: founder story (first person, plain language), certification explanation paragraph, team section intro.
7. Write FAQ page: 8 questions minimum covering cost, timeline, insurance claims, certifications, warranty, and the difference between repair and replacement.

## Pass criteria

- Hero headline begins with a quantified proof signal (review count, certification tier, or years in business) — not a vague claim.
- Zero instances of blocklisted AI-vocab terms (see references/copy/ai-vocab-blocklist.md for the full list).
- Every testimonial includes: full name, city and state, specific outcome sentence (not just a star rating).
- Insurance claim assistance is mentioned in hero subhead or hero form area — not buried below the fold.
- Submit button text is exactly "Get My Free Quote" on every form instance.

## When this stage halts

- brand-dna.json has no review count and no certifications: halt, request client to provide current Google review count and any manufacturer certifications before copy can be written.
- Company has zero named testimonials and no before/after photos: flag to student. Copy can proceed but Reviews section will render placeholder text; student must supply real testimonials before delivery.

## Niche-specific notes

For roofing contractors, the single most conversion-critical copy element is the review count in the hero. Homeowners filter contractors by review volume before they read anything else. The hero headline should lead with a number: "312 Five-Star Reviews" or "Serving Atlanta Since 2009 — 200+ Verified Reviews." This is not optional for this niche.

The insurance claim angle is an underused differentiator. Any roofing contractor who handles insurance claims should have that stated in the hero subhead or as a trust chip. Copy: "We handle the insurance claim for you" — plain, specific, no jargon.

Named testimonials outperform star ratings 3:1 in this niche. Every testimonial must include: full name (not initials), city and state, and a sentence describing a specific outcome ("They replaced our roof in one day and saved us $2,400 on the insurance deductible").
