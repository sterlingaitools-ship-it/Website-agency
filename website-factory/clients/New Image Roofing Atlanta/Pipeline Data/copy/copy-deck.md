**Q: How do I know if I have a leak before I see water inside?**

**H1:**
Gutter Installation and Cleaning in Atlanta, GA

**Hero paragraph (64 words):**
Gutters do one job: move water away from your home. When they clog, pull away from the fascia, or fail at the seams, water backs up against your foundation, soaks your siding, and floods your basement. New Image Roofing Atlanta installs, repairs, and cleans gutters across Metro-Atlanta. Free inspection with any roofing estimate. We install seamless aluminum and steel systems with proper pitch and secure fastening.

**H2 1: What Gutter Services Cover**
We offer: seamless gutter installation (aluminum and steel, in multiple widths and colors), gutter repair (rehanging, seam sealing, endcap replacement), downspout installation and extension, gutter cleaning (full removal of debris with flush-down test), and gutter guard installation to reduce cleaning frequency. We assess your existing system at the free inspection and tell you exactly what it needs.

**H2 2: What to Expect**
For gutter installation: We measure the full perimeter, confirm pitch and drainage points, and fabricate seamless sections on-site. Installation of a typical home's gutters takes half a day to one full day. For gutter cleaning: We remove all debris by hand, flush all sections and downspouts with water, and confirm proper flow at each downspout exit. We report any damage found during cleaning. For gutter repair: We identify the source of the failure (improper pitch, fastener failure, seam separation) and fix the cause, not just the symptom.

**H2 3: Materials We Use**
Standard installation uses .027-gauge aluminum in 5-inch K-style profile. We also offer 6-inch K-style for high-volume drainage areas and steel gutters for commercial applications. Seamless sections are fabricated on-site from coil stock in over 30 colors. Downspouts are standard 2x3 or 3x4 rectangular aluminum. All fasteners are hidden spike-free clips that will not pull out over time.

**H2 4: Warranty**
Gutter installations come with a workmanship warranty covering the installation. Manufacturer warranties apply to materials. Gutter cleaning is not a warrantied service but we note any new damage found and quote repairs before we leave.

**H2 5: Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: How often should gutters be cleaned in Atlanta?**
A: Most Atlanta homeowners with tree coverage need cleaning twice a year: spring (after pollen and seed drop) and fall (after leaves). Homes with heavy tree coverage may need three cleanings per year.

**Q: What is the difference between seamless and sectional gutters?**
A: Sectional gutters are cut to length and joined with seamed connectors. Every seam is a potential leak point. Seamless gutters are fabricated in one continuous piece to fit your roofline. They have far fewer leak points and last longer. New Image installs seamless.

**Q: Can I get my gutters cleaned at the same time as a roof inspection?**
A: Yes. We offer gutter cleaning as an add-on to any roof inspection or maintenance visit.

**Q: How do I know if my gutters need to be replaced rather than repaired?**
A: If gutters have separated from the fascia along most of their run, have widespread rust, or are severely dented and misshapen, replacement is more cost-effective than repair. If the failure is limited to specific sections, repair is often the right call. We give you an honest assessment at the inspection.

**Q: Do you install gutter guards?**
A: Yes. We offer micro-mesh gutter guards that significantly reduce debris entry. They do not eliminate the need for cleaning but extend the interval between cleanings. We can quote guards as part of any installation or add them to existing gutters.

### Category: Insurance Claims

**Q: How do I know if my roof damage is covered by insurance?**
A: Most homeowner's insurance policies cover storm damage from hail and wind. Wear, age, and poor maintenance are not covered. The key is having proper documentation of the damage and its cause. We photograph and document everything. Call us before you file. It makes the process faster and the outcome better for you.

**Q: Do you work with insurance companies?**
A: Yes. We have worked with every major insurer serving the Atlanta market. We know what adjusters look for and how to document claims so they are approved at the right number. We attend the adjuster meeting if you want us there.

**Q: What if the insurance company only wants to repair and I think it needs full replacement?**
A: This comes up regularly. We document our scope with photos and a written report. If the adjuster's scope is insufficient, we provide a supplemental claim with our documentation. We do not accept an underpaid scope without pushing back. We are working for you, not for the insurer.

### Category: About Your Roof

**Q: How long should a roof last in Atlanta?**
A: Standard three-tab shingles last 15-20 years in Atlanta's climate. Architectural shingles last 25-30 years. Premium impact-resistant shingles can last 30-50 years. Heat, UV exposure, and Atlanta's storm frequency all accelerate wear compared to cooler climates. Annual inspection after year 10 is a sensible practice.

**Q: What are the signs I need a new roof?**
A: Look for: shingles that are cracked, curling, or missing, granules collecting in gutters (they look like coarse black sand), visible sagging or uneven roof lines, stains on ceilings or walls inside, and daylight visible through the attic. Any of these warrants a professional inspection.
